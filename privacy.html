Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

1. Overview

PROMPT is a video teleprompter app for creating scripts, recording videos and editing recordings. PROMPT is designed to work locally, without requiring an account. We do not collect your scripts, videos or voice recordings.

2. Scripts and recordings

Your scripts, videos and editing data are stored on your device. PROMPT does not upload them to our servers. If you choose to share or export content to another app or service, that destination handles the content under its own privacy practices. Device backups, if enabled, are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

3. Camera and microphone

PROMPT requests camera and microphone access to record videos. You control these permissions through iOS Settings. Camera and microphone content is used for the recording functions you choose to use.

4. Speech recognition

Voice Follow uses Apple's on-device speech recognition to follow the words you speak. PROMPT requests speech recognition permission when needed. Voice Follow is configured to require on-device recognition; your microphone audio is not sent to PROMPT servers for transcription. This feature may be unavailable on devices or languages without supported on-device recognition.

5. Purchases

Optional PROMPT Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. PROMPT can receive purchase and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features. Apple handles payment information under its own policies; PROMPT does not need your full card details.

6. Analytics and third parties

PROMPT does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs. This website does not use cookies for tracking or include analytics scripts. GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, may process standard technical request information according to GitHub's own policies. Apple may independently provide diagnostic or App Store information under Apple's policies.

7. Data control and deletion

You can manage your locally saved scripts and recordings in PROMPT. Removing the app may delete data stored only on your device. Export any recordings you want to keep before uninstalling. For help with data questions, contact support.

8. Children's privacy

PROMPT is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been sent to us, please contact support.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as PROMPT changes. The effective date above will be updated when the policy is revised.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact prompt@kook.love.
