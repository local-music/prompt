Support

Quick answers to common questions about PROMPT.

How do I use the teleprompter?

Create a script, open it in the teleprompter and adjust the text size or scrolling speed to suit your recording. You can also scroll manually.

Why isn't my camera or microphone working?

Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera or Microphone and make sure PROMPT has permission. Then reopen the app.

What is Voice Follow?

Voice Follow is a PROMPT Pro feature that uses speech recognition to help the teleprompter follow what you say. You may need to allow speech recognition access. Availability and accuracy can depend on your device, language and environment.

How do I restore PROMPT Pro?

Open PROMPT's Pro or subscription screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Subscription management is available in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Where are my scripts and videos?

PROMPT is designed to keep scripts and saved recordings on your device. Check the app's scripts and recordings sections. Deleting the app may remove locally stored content, so export important recordings first.
